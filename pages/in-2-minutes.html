Just a Minute.

The one-minute route

Six ideas. One connected system.

This is a brief introduction to the Cognitif experience. It takes about one minute, moving from what people say to how collective sentiment changes over time—before you explore the full platform in detail.

  1. 01

    Traditional surveys measure what individual people say.

  2. 02

    Petalyx™ measures what people instinctively mean.

  3. 03

    Ontologos converts those associations into stable geometric coordinates.

  4. 04

    Semantic Bathymetry™ transforms them into anonymised population data.

  5. 05

    Semba independently protects that dataset.

  6. 06

    Insightvault analyses collective sentiment and how it changes over time.

The principle

True semantic orientation requires implicit topography.

The behavioural shift

Rather than eliciting conscious ratings, we present a forced-choice word-association task embedded within a carefully constructed semantic pool.

The mechanism

Orientation is inferred from the pattern of split-second selections and rejections across 50 trials. The profile emerges from involuntary behavioural navigation rather than curated declaration.

The outcome

A scalable, standardised method resistant to surface distortion.
