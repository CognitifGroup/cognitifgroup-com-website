Petalyx™ — Your Privacy

Who we are

Petalyx™ is the data capture interface of Cognitif Group Limited. We run a word-association exercise to understand how people feel about abstract ideas — things like trust, authority, or motivation. We do this by measuring which words you associate with other words, and how quickly you respond. Petalyx™ is a proprietary data capture architecture owned and operated by Cognitif Group Limited as part of the Cognitif.ai software suite.

What we collect

  • Which word you selected from the options shown.
  • How long it took you to respond (in milliseconds, relative to when the word appeared). This timing is adjusted on your device before anything leaves it.
  • Optionally, your age group and gender — but only if you choose to provide them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect — and cannot collect — your name, email address, IP address, location, device type, or any other information that could identify you. No login is required. No cookies are used.

How your data is used

Your responses are immediately converted into anonymous numerical values before leaving your device. The words themselves are never stored or transmitted. We use the anonymised data to build population-level maps of how groups of people relate to abstract concepts. We do not build individual profiles and we do not make decisions about you based on your responses.

Who sees your data

Your anonymised responses are processed within the Cognitif.ai platform operated by Cognitif Group Limited. The Semba.ai dataset — the anonymised population-level corpus — is owned and governed by SEMBA AI, a Company Limited by Guarantee with an independent governance board. Only aggregated, population-level insights are shared with our business partners (licensees) via the Insightvault.ai visualisation portal. Your individual session data is never sold, shared, or disclosed.

Your choices

You can stop at any time. Participation is entirely voluntary.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any information that identifies you, the standard data access and deletion rights under UK data protection law cannot be exercised against your session — there is simply nothing linked to you to find, correct, or delete. If you have questions about how we handle data, you can contact our Data Protection Officer at andrew@cognitifgroup.ai.

Oversight

Our anonymisation process is independently audited annually. The Semba.ai dataset is owned and governed by SEMBA AI, a Company Limited by Guarantee constituted with a majority of independent Non-Executive Directors.
